How we report results

  • Every number comes from an official results report (the county or the Secretary of State), linked under each contest.
  • Candidates are listed in alphabetical order, the same as everywhere on this site, never by vote count.
  • We mark a candidate elected, or a proposition passed or failed, only after the official canvass.
  • A race that crosses county lines may show a partial count; the page says which voters it covers.
