What counts

The record
The House Clerk's roll-call file or the Senate's vote file: the question, the result, and how each member voted, as printed.
Party and state
Exactly as the roll call lists each member. The House Clerk also prints totals by party; we show them as printed. The Senate's record prints none, so we show none.
Names and portraits
Full names and official portraits come from the public-domain congress-legislators and images projects, every member the same size, served from this site.
What doesn't count
News reports, ratings, scorecards, or anyone's guess about why a member voted as they did. A roll call records each vote, not the reasons for it.
