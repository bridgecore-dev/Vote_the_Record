{# The Capitol switchboard and a neutral "what to say" script. The same for every member and every vote. #}

Call Congress: (202) 224-3121

The U.S. Capitol switchboard connects you to any member's office; ask for them by name. Not sure who represents you? Find your representative (if it shows two, enter your full street address: your ZIP code crosses a district line) or your senators.

Never called before? Here's what to say
  1. Say your name and where you live. Offices pay most attention to their own voters, so give your city or ZIP code.
  2. Name the vote: the bill number or the roll call, and the date.
  3. Say what you think, or ask how the member voted or will vote and why. Stick to one topic and keep it to a minute or two.
  4. A staff member or voicemail will answer. That's normal: offices write down every call. After hours, leave the same message on voicemail.
